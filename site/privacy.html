The privacy promise

Your history stays yours.

MuseAi YouTube Rewinds is designed around a simple idea: a personal archive should not become someone else’s dataset.

Plain English

No upload-shaped surprises.

The rewind CLI reads your local Google Takeout files and writes local Markdown, JSON, CSV, and playlist outputs. The site does not ask for a Google login, ship your history anywhere, scrape YouTube, or store API credentials.

The optional channel tools are separate. If you use them, credentials belong in environment variables, not in the repository. Editing defaults to dry-run and requires an explicit `--apply`.
